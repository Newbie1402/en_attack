# 01 — Detailed game design

## Product pillars
Fun first, math as a weapon, time as a resource. Single-player desktop-first game; Vietnamese UI by default, English optional. Working title SnowGraph. Original characters; approved visual references in `references/`. Players defend a nest village at the left from wildlife moving right-to-left. Snowballs remain the signature attack in every season. No graphic violence.

## State and board
Match: ready -> running <-> paused -> won/lost. Ready permits placement using starting energy, and explicit Start. Only running advances combat, nests, pickups, cooldowns and skill effects. Pause via UI/Esc, hidden tab pauses without auto-resume. Result overlay, retry from fresh seed/state, quit confirmation. Victory after all waves spawn and all enemies are defeated; base loses one heart per enemy breach, enemy removed; at zero hearts lose, loss wins simultaneous tie. Default 3 hearts. Board default 10 columns x 5 rows, square cells, 0-based cell IDs; first three columns deployment, configurable per level. One placed unit per tile, walkable/blocked/deployable flags. No physical perspective distortion in tile centers. Decorations outside board or non-occluding. Enemies normally follow lane center, can switch lanes via authored motion. Defenders don't body-block enemies in MVP. Obstacles block projectile path, not enemy lanes unless explicit routing configured.

## Units and economy
Snow Swallow (basic), Frost Swallow (on-hit slow), Heavy Swallow (area impact), Lucky Nest (production). Select unit card, then click valid tile to place; validate cost and occupancy atomically. Select placed swallow to aim. Move selected bird via Move then destination, cost/cooldown and deployment constraints; cooldown and pending next-shot boost persist. Active shots retain immutable origin and path even if bird moves. Move cannot occur while placement mode active. Nest stationary. No selling in MVP. Starting inventory/energy per level, energy pickups +25 default; no passive income except configured emergency grant. Cap energy at 999. Prevent negative balances and duplicate collection. Unit stats in 10; no shooting by nests. Keyboard and mouse/touch alternatives. No automatic formula entry except explicit tutorial hint or sandbox selection.

## Aiming and combat
Local origin at selected bird cell center. Hover targets/tile to see relative coordinates rounded to 0.1 cells, plus up/down sign guide in tutorial. No axes during campaign. Formula typed in bottom editor; enter/fire commits one shot if valid, selected bird available, cooldown ready, quota available and capacity available. Per-bird cooldown, all-player quota. Preview is visual hint, does not predict enemy future position or guarantee hit. Fire can be repeated manually with different birds; no autocast. Basic pierces max 2 enemies, frost max 1, heavy stops on first contact with radial AOE (one hit per enemy). All stop at obstacle or board edge. Armor checks trajectory tangent direction at hit (top/bottom means steep in board plane, not actual height). AOE does not bypass armor unless specified. See math engine. Damage snapshot on launch; impact feedback and enemy death animations do not delay removal from domain.

## Lucky Nest: production and collection
Separate deterministic production timers for energy and skills; timers run only when match running, unaffected by enemy time control. Seeded RNG based on level weighted skill table, with pity for boss/reset. Every production event creates a domain pickup id with owner/type, bounded target tile near nest, spawn tick, collectible tick, expire tick. Select nearest available non-obstacle tile in deterministic order, then seeded tie break; may share tile with a defender/enemy but draw offset and accessible hit area. Maximum one uncollected skill per nest; global max 12 pickups; if cap reached, defer production (do not silently mint stock). Visual stages: glow/shake/crack before spawn; pop/arc/bounce during first ~0.6s; then hover collectible; expire after 12s collectible; blink in last 2s. Energy analogous, with distinct visuals. Domain pickup exists during pop but not collectable until ready. Click/tap or keyboard focus + collect. Atomic CollectPickup validates state, expiry, cap, credits stock/energy once, removes pickup, emits event for fly-to-HUD. Animation never changes inventory. Full inventory leaves skill on field until space/expiry (show full); energy cap accepts remaining capacity and shows actual credited amount, if full leaves on field. Expiry removes it. No auto-collect default. Accessibility setting reduces animation motion, not timing or rewards.

## Tactical skills
- Slow: global enemy movement and future enemy attack timers x0.4 for 8 seconds; refresh duration, no multiplicative stacking. Does not slow waves, nests, projectiles or player cooldowns.
- Freeze: global enemy movement/attack x0 for 4 seconds; overrides Slow while active, both timers tick in game time; enemies spawned while active affected. Boss freeze resistance optional authored, explicitly shown, MVP no resistance. Freeze consumes skill only when successful; shared 2s cooldown across time skills and same-type effect may refresh only if permitted. Max stock 3 each.
- Double Damage: arm next accepted shot globally (indicator in HUD); snapshot x2 base damage; no stacking, consumes stock when armed, rejected fire preserves armed effect. Persists until shot or level ends.
- Formula Reset: choose registered used bucket; restore count to zero only for that bucket; no family reset. Disabled when no used bucket, invalid selection never consumes stock. Global across birds and phases. Max stock 2.
- Optional post-MVP Free Move (one successful free relocation), Perfect Vision (10s reveal preview on restricted levels). Do not expose unusable controls in MVP.

## Enemies and boss
Snow Fox baseline, Ice Boar armor frontal (incoming from left with shallow tangent), Shadow Raven switches lanes, Frost Guardian bear boss. Boss health phases: >70% create telegraphed obstacles at preauthored valid cells; 30–70% inclusive upper threshold activates formula exact cap 3; <=30% also limits preview (off by default challenge), speeds up and summons adds. Thresholds triggered once, in order even when a single hit crosses both. Track accepted formula uses from beginning of quota-enabled boss match, including pre-lock phases; UI displays lock activates at threshold and existing uses count. Earlier shots may leave a bucket already locked when phase changes. Boss and scripted adds share wave completion condition. Phase summons are tracked in scheduler before win evaluation. Boss armor does not imply real 3D altitude. No unavoidable attacks or RNG-only win conditions. See 10 for provisional tuning and pity rules.

## Campaign and sandbox
MVP six authored levels: spring garden L1 straight, L2 placement/production, L3 quadratic obstacle; winter L4 trig/time skills, L5 quotas/reset, L6 boss. Unlock next on win; replay cleared; tutorial skip/replay. Full content target 6 chapters x (5 regular + boss) =36, Spring Garden, Summer Coast, Autumn Woods, Winter Valley, Sky Islands, Frost Kingdom. Content packs separate art/config, no engine fork. World map visually styled as journey; locked levels readable. Sandbox independent from campaign: choose supported formula, place units/targets freely, toggle preview and optional axes, reset, no resource/cooldown limits by default, no progression credit. Settings volume, mute, reduced motion, hints, locale; bestiary describes all implemented units, skills and formula examples. Local save only versioned progress/settings, no in-progress match resume in MVP.
