# SnowGraph V3 art direction

The approved direction remains polished hand-illustrated 2D cartoon art: expressive En Việt pilot swallows,
warm daylight, controlled spring/winter palettes, clean silhouettes, consistent navy/orange/cream accents,
soft environmental shadows and readable gameplay contrast. References are direction only; they are never
used as runtime screenshots or sprites.

The V3 slice extends local original SVG art with rabbit, turtle and wolf silhouettes and renders placement
ghosts, selection rings, armor bars and blocked-hit feedback. The existing SVGs are still first-pass
production-local illustrations, not a commissioned atlas or final audio package. Those remain an explicit
asset-production follow-up rather than a false visual pass.
