# 04 — Approved UI and art direction

## Source of truth
Inspect `references/mascot-original.png`, `references/gameplay-concept-01.png`, `references/gameplay-concept-02.png`. These are visual references, not production sprites or exact gameplay rules. The concept depicts warm wood/parchment UI, detailed garden/ice tiles, flowers, mascot cards, luminous skill drops, snowball trails and enemies. Preserve character identity, craftsmanship and visual hierarchy; reduce clutter and glow versus concept where needed. Do not recreate any other game's proprietary artwork. Reference logos are owner's provided visual material; confirm permission before public branded release; do not invent or distort logos. No generated text baked into assets.

## Art direction and tokens
Bright seasonal 2D with 2.5D-style shading, orthographic square board (no isometric projection). Hand-painted/cel-shaded cartoon, expressive silhouettes, warm sunlight, soft contact shadows, natural scenery. Mascot navy #26354D, orange #F97316, cream #FFF5E5; grass #A8C982, ice #C6E5F3, wood #B99570; ink #26354D, muted ink #536474. Actual map palette varies by season. Warm wood/parchment panels, subtle texture, limited bevel and selective outlines. Use readable licensed font with Vietnamese diacritics; math monospace or clean math font. No SaaS cards, giant headings, generic gradient backgrounds, purple-blue neon, glassmorphism, emoji sprites, excessive blur/shadow/borders, random decoration. Skill colors distinct but paired with shape/icon and text; restrained glow only for collectible/action state. No text rasterized in HUD art.

## Layout
Desktop 1440x900 / 1920x1080: top compact brand, resource, wave bar, hearts, pause/settings; left narrow illustrated unit shop; central board largest, square 10x5 tiles, themed landscape surrounding; bottom parchment formula editor and Fire action; bottom/right skill inventory. No permanent Ox/Oy, labels or chart axes. Select tile bright outline; target hover shows local coordinate bubble; snow-dotted path preview with minimal glow, disappears on cancel/invalid/hidden. Optional tutorial counts and y-up arrow outside board; Sandbox optional axes. The UI should feel like a game, not spreadsheet. On 1280x720 all gameplay controls visible; fit board by same uniform scale; at narrow widths stack toolbar/skills and support landscape touch or show friendly rotate suggestion (desktop-first, no forced zoom). Support 390x844 menu/setting pages, touch gameplay best effort; no hidden essential controls. High-DPI canvas capped DPR by performance policy; resize preserves game coordinates.

## Screen specifications
Main menu: illustrated world and mascot, Continue/New Game/Campaign/Sandbox/Settings/Bestiary; confirmation before deleting progress. Campaign: illustrated journey nodes and locks, 6 chapters eventual, unavailable content clearly marked, no fake levels. Play: shop card price/disabled reason, selected unit/cooldown, formula text with keyboard helpers, explicit error, Fire enabled only when legal (domain revalidates), quota remaining and reset picker when active, preview switch obeys level, skill stock/effect timer, pause and result overlay. Sandbox: same board renderer, freely placed targets, optional grid/axes, unlimited test actions; not a separate math implementation. Bestiary: actual unit portraits and mechanics. Settings: locale, audio, motion, hints. All labels Vietnamese first with English dictionary, text selectable where appropriate, readable contrasts.

## Animation direction
Bird idle breathe/blink, wing flutter, anticipation 100–150ms, release synced to domain ShotFired (cosmetic anticipation may start on input, never delay actual collision), recoil/recovery, move hop and land, victory flap. Projectile snowball spin, snow trail, impact squash/burst; damage float short fade; hit flash and death poof. Enemy walk, armor hit, frozen tint/crystals, raven flight, boss telegraph. Lucky Nest visible egg glow/wiggle/crack; pickup pop arc, rotation, bounce, idle float, click -> fly to HUD -> stock pulse. Domain has already credited collection; visual follows event even if panel anim interrupted. Weather petals, leaves, snow, water shimmer outside board; few particles, reduced motion disables nonessential effects. Do not show preview and actual shot as indistinguishable trails. All feedback must be legible without sound or color alone. See asset pipeline for frame/manifest rules.
