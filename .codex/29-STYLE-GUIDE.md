# SnowGraph illustrated style guide

## Locked direction

Use bright, hand-illustrated chibi 2D cartoon art: warm daylight, expressive silhouettes, soft shadows and
clear square battlefield cells. The approved swallow family keeps a navy pilot cap, navy/blue feathers,
cream belly, orange scarf/accents, forked tail and large expressive eyes.

## Palette and craft rules

- Spring: leaf greens, cream paper, orange warmth, restrained coral flowers and navy outlines.
- Winter: frosted blue-green, snow white, muted navy and small warm-gold accents for affordances.
- Outline characters consistently with a dark navy/blue line; use a lighter interior shadow, not a black
  sticker border.
- Regular entities occupy roughly 0.7–0.9 of a cell; bosses may overlap visually but never change collision.
- Keep tile variation handcrafted and low contrast so enemies, formula preview and pickups remain legible.
- Use rounded sans UI text with Vietnamese glyph coverage; use monospace only for formulas or numeric data.
- Never use a permanent Cartesian axis on campaign gameplay; local math context belongs to the selected
  swallow/editor state.

## Forbidden

No pixel-art pivot, unrelated mascot styles, giant dashboard cards, screenshot-as-background gameplay,
hotlinked art, or “complete” labels for files that are not actually present and reviewed.
